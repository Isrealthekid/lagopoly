LAGOS PROPERTY GAME - LOCAL RULES, ASSETS, AND CARDS
==================================================
Version 1.1 - location groups and price bands revised

1. THE LAGOS EDITION
-------------------
Become the last solvent investor by acquiring Lagos district assets,
operating BRT transport assets, managing landmark venues, developing
complete property groups, and collecting income from visiting players.

This is a fictional game economy. Owning a space represents a game
investment or operating concession, not ownership of a whole community
or a real public building. Prices, fees, taxes, and events are invented
for play. The board is not a geographical map or a transport timetable.

The base rules retain the familiar property-trading structure. Local
adaptations include naira currency, BRT assets instead of railroads,
landmark-service assets instead of power/water utilities, automatic
income collection, and Lagos-themed events.

2. PLAYERS, BANK, AND STARTING CONDITIONS
---------------------------------------
- 2-4 players in the initial web version; all start at Eko Start.
- Each starts with N1,500,000. N means Nigerian naira throughout.
- Two six-sided dice; 32 development units and 12 flagship upgrades.
- Separate 16-card Community Chest and Chance decks, shuffled once.
- The game bank holds all unsold assets and processes transactions.
- Bank cash is unlimited; the development-piece supply is limited.
- Everyone rolls for starting order. Tied highest rollers reroll until
  one wins; continue clockwise through the player list from that player.
- Normal victory: the last player not declared bankrupt wins.

4. TURNS, DICE, AND MOVEMENT
--------------------------
Roll, move clockwise by the dice total, then resolve the space completely.
Players may occupy the same position. Collect N200,000 from the bank
when passing or landing on Eko Start through forward movement.
An exact landing gives N200,000, not twice that amount.

Doubles allow another roll after resolving the landing. On a third
consecutive double in the same turn, go straight to Holding before making
that roll's normal move. Reset the doubles count when the turn ends.
Any direct Holding instruction immediately ends the turn.

After mandatory effects are settled, manage assets and finish the turn
or take an earned extra roll. No rolling or ending a turn while a debt,
auction, card effect, or required decision remains unresolved.

5. BUYING AND AUCTIONS
---------------------
On an unowned purchasable space, buy from the bank at the listed price.
If you decline or cannot afford it, an immediate auction follows.
All solvent players may bid, including the player who declined.
Opening/minimum bid is N1,000; subsequent bids increase by at least
N1,000. This bidding increment is a local implementation choice.
Bids must be backed by cash available at the time of bidding.
Highest remaining bidder pays the bank and takes ownership.
If everyone passes without bidding, the asset remains with the bank.

6. GROUP-ASSET INCOME AND DEVELOPMENT
------------------------------------
Land on another player's unmortgaged asset and pay its scheduled income
automatically. Land on your own asset and pay nothing.
The whole colour group doubles the BASE fee of its undeveloped assets.
Developed assets use their printed fee, without another doubling.
Mortgaged assets earn nothing. An unmortgaged undeveloped asset still
gets the group bonus if its owner holds every asset in the group.

Each development unit represents residential/commercial improvement.

Own a complete, unmortgaged group to develop it. Build evenly: asset
levels in a group differ by at most one. A flagship counts as level 5.
Maximum: four units or one flagship per asset. To upgrade, all assets
in the group must have at least four units; pay one UNIT COST and return
that asset's four units to the bank in exchange for a flagship.
No development on BRT or landmark-service assets.

Development transactions occur between resolved actions, including
between players' turns; required debts and auctions take priority.
Limited pieces cannot be replaced by imaginary units. If competing
players request more available pieces than the bank has, auction those
pieces using section 5's bidding rules.

7. BRT TRANSPORT ASSETS
----------------------
Ikorodu Garage BRT, Yaba BRT, Marina BRT, and TBS BRT each cost N200,000.
The landing fee depends on how many BRT assets the owner holds:
1 = N25,000; 2 = N50,000; 3 = N100,000; 4 = N200,000.
Mortgaged assets count toward ownership totals but earn no landing fee.
No new buildings or additional dice rolls apply to ordinary BRT landings.
Chance can specify twice the ordinary fee; it never charges on a mortgage.

8. LANDMARK-SERVICE ASSETS: THE TWO UTILITY SLOTS
-----------------------------------------------
National Theatre and Tafawa Balewa Square each cost N150,000.
They provide fictional cultural/event services, with a variable usage
fee replacing the classic utility charge. They are not power or water
utilities; this is an intentional Lagos game adaptation.

If the opponent owns one landmark, pay arrival dice total x N4,000.
If the opponent owns both, pay arrival dice total x N10,000.
Example: a total of 7 costs N28,000 or N70,000 respectively.
Count both holdings even if the other landmark is mortgaged; charge
nothing if the destination itself is mortgaged.
For a card arrival without a specified fee, roll a fresh fee-only total.
A fee-only roll never moves the token or earns an extra roll.
CH07 explicitly uses a fresh total x N10,000 regardless of holding count.

9. MORTGAGES, SALES, AND TRADES
-----------------------------
Sell all development in a group before mortgaging any of its assets or
transferring any of its assets to another player.
Sell units back for half their UNIT COST, removing levels evenly.
Downgrade a flagship to four units by receiving half its upgrade cost
and taking four bank units; this requires sufficient available pieces.
Alternatively liquidate all development in that group in one transaction:
receive half each unit's cost, and half five UNIT COSTs for each flagship.
This group-liquidation option avoids a shortage preventing debt payment.

Mortgaging pays half the asset's purchase price; ownership stays with you.
To redeem, pay that principal plus 10%. Example: a N200,000 BRT asset
pays N100,000 when mortgaged and costs N110,000 to redeem.
No player can redeem someone else's mortgage to claim their asset.

Players may exchange cash, eligible assets, and unused release cards.
No loans, rent immunity agreements, or promises of future payments.
Trades need both players' acceptance and are executed atomically.
Recheck cash, ownership, and mortgage charges when accepting a trade.
An asset transfer with a mortgage requires its recipient to either:
- Pay principal plus 10% immediately and redeem it; or
- Pay 10% immediately, retain the mortgage, and later pay principal
  plus another 10% when redeeming it.
Transfers are not allowed while a mandatory debt remains unresolved.
However, the debtor may offer asset sales to raise cash for that debt.

10. HOLDING, LEVIES, AND OWAMBE BREAK
-----------------------------------
Holding is a fictional game detention space, not a named real facility.
Enter through Go to Holding, a card, or the third consecutive double.
Move directly to position 10, end the turn, and collect no Start reward
for that transfer. Previously earned Start money is not taken back.
Ordinary landing at 10 is Just Visiting and has no charge.

On your turn in Holding, choose one of these before rolling:
- Pay N50,000, then take a normal roll and turn.
- Use a retained release card, then take a normal roll and turn.
- Attempt doubles. Success releases you and moves by that total, with
  no extra roll. Failure leaves you there and ends that turn.
After the third failed attempt, pay N50,000 and move by that attempt's
total. If unable to pay, resolve debt before movement.
You may still receive income and perform permitted asset transactions.

Personal Income Levy costs N200,000; Premium Asset Levy costs N100,000.
These fixed game charges do not represent actual Lagos tax regulations.
Owambe Break is a free rest space: no jackpot or extra turn.

11. DEBT, BANKRUPTCY, AND VICTORY
-------------------------------
When cash is insufficient, pause normal play and allow the debtor to
sell eligible assets, sell development, or mortgage assets. No new
rolls or purchases until the obligation is settled.
If the debtor cannot raise enough, declare bankruptcy.
Debt to a player: return development for its half-cost proceeds, then
transfer remaining cash, assets, and release cards to the creditor.
The recipient resolves mortgage-transfer charges before play resumes;
if unable, that recipient also enters debt resolution.
Debt to the bank: return development and cards, remove mortgages,
and auction each returned asset individually. The player is eliminated.
For multi-player card transfers, settle opponents in clockwise order
from the drawer; any bankruptcy is resolved against the current creditor.
Return bank-recovered release cards to the bottom of their original decks.
With one solvent player remaining, show that player's victory immediately.

12. CARD PROCEDURE
------------------
Community Chest represents neighbourhood support and everyday expenses.
Chance represents movement, opportunities, delays, and unexpected costs.
Draw the top card, apply its entire effect, and return it to the bottom.
Keep release cards out of circulation until used or transferred.
Do not reshuffle after each draw. The two decks remain separate.

All receipts/payments are with the bank unless an opponent is named.
Forward movement collects N200,000 if it crosses or reaches Start.
A card moving you to Start awards exactly N200,000 for that move, once.
Direct Holding transfers and backward movement award no Start money.
Resolve the destination after movement, including purchases, auctions,
income, levies, or another card draw. A retained card is returned to
its source deck only when used or recovered by the bank.
For repair cards, a flagship counts only as a flagship, not four units.

15. WEB GAME REQUIREMENTS AND RULE CHECKS
---------------------------------------
Show the location, group, owner, price, fee table, mortgage, and development
on each asset's detail panel. Use full names in details and shorter board
labels where necessary. Keep group colours accompanied by written labels.
Distinguish venue usage fees, transport fees, and district rent in logs.
Bank payments and opponent payments must identify their recipients.

Store positions, deck order, retained cards, cash, ownership, mortgages,
piece supply, doubles count, Holding attempts, and pending decisions.
Save after every accepted action. Restore unresolved debts and auctions.
No dice animation, refresh, or repeated click may apply a payment twice.

Required checks before calling this edition playable:
- Exactly 40 positions; all 28 purchasable assets have valid data.
- All requested locations, landmarks, and transport labels appear.
- Colour-group membership follows section 3, including the revised
  three-member Yellow/Green and two-member Brown/Dark Blue sets.
- Complete groups double only undeveloped income.
- BRT and landmark fee overrides respect mortgages and ownership.
- Moving back from Chance 36 draws Community Chest at 33.
- Forward cards across Start award once per qualifying movement.
- Holding release rolls and fee-only rolls never earn extra rolls.
- Development and liquidation preserve the limited-piece inventory.
- Trades cannot spend cash twice or bypass group-building restrictions.
- Bankruptcy and creditor mortgage charges can complete without deadlock.
- Desktop/mobile layouts, save restoration, and full games work.
