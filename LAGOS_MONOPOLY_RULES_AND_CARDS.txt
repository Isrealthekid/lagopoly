CLASSIC MONOPOLY RULES AND CARDS - LAGOS DEVELOPMENT REFERENCE
============================================================

SCOPE
-----
This is a plain-language rules summary and a compact description of card
effects, not a reproduction of the official rulebook or printed cards.
The card baseline is the traditional US Classic deck used in 2008-2021.
Later Community Chest decks and other country editions differ.
Dollar amounts below are game money, retained for reference.
Lagos locations and naira values have not yet been assigned.

1. OBJECTIVE, EQUIPMENT, AND SETUP
--------------------------------
Win by being the last player who has not gone bankrupt.
Use a 40-space board, two dice, tokens, property deeds, money,
32 houses, 12 hotels, and separate 16-card Chance and Community Chest
decks. Shuffle each deck separately and place it facedown.
Each player starts on GO with $1,500. The bank holds unsold assets and
handles payments and auctions; its funds are separate from player funds.
The bank does not run out of money.
Roll to determine the starting player; play proceeds clockwise.

2. MOVEMENT AND BUYING
---------------------
Roll both dice, move clockwise by their total, and resolve the destination.
Tokens may share spaces. Passing or landing on GO normally awards $200,
not $400 for an exact landing.
Doubles grant another roll after resolving the landing. A third
consecutive double sends you directly to Jail before moving normally.
Being sent to Jail ends the turn and awards no GO payment for that move.
Buy an unowned property at its listed price or auction it immediately.
Everyone, including the player who declined, may bid. Highest bidder pays
the bank and receives ownership.

3. RENT AND SPECIAL ASSETS
-------------------------
Pay the owner when landing on an opponent's unmortgaged property.
Use its deed's rent schedule. Complete colour groups double undeveloped
rent; developed properties use their building rent instead.
Railroad rent is $25/$50/$100/$200 for ownership of 1/2/3/4 railroads.
Utility rent is 4 times the arrival dice total for one utility, or 10 times
for both. Certain Chance cards override these calculations.
Mortgaged properties earn no rent. Other unmortgaged properties in a
complete group still qualify for doubled undeveloped rent.
The traditional rent-claim deadline is before the next dice roll.

4. BUILDINGS AND TRADING
-----------------------
Own the entire colour group, with no mortgages in it, before building.
Buy buildings from the bank at deed prices. Build evenly: development
levels within a group may differ by at most one house.
After four houses on every property in a group, replace four houses on
a chosen property with one hotel and pay its listed upgrade cost.
Maximum per property: four houses or one hotel. No buildings on railroads
or utilities. Building is permitted on your turn or between turns.
The physical building supply is limited; competing demands for scarce
buildings are settled by auction.
Sell buildings to the bank at half cost, reducing development evenly.
A fully sold hotel returns half its upgrade cost plus half the cost of
the four houses exchanged for it.
Players may negotiate sales/trades of cash, undeveloped properties, and
release cards. Remove every building in a colour group before trading
a property from that group. Player-to-player loans are not allowed.

5. MORTGAGES AND BANKRUPTCY
--------------------------
Sell all buildings in a group before mortgaging a property in it.
Receive the mortgage amount printed on its deed; retain ownership.
Repay principal plus 10% to restore rent collection.
Under traditional mortgage-transfer rules, a recipient either repays
principal plus 10% immediately, or pays 10% immediately and retains the
mortgage. Later redemption then costs principal plus another 10%.
Attempt permitted asset sales and mortgages before declaring bankruptcy.
If still unable to settle a debt, leave the game.
For debt to another player, sell buildings back at half cost and transfer
remaining money, properties, and release cards to that creditor.
Mortgage-transfer charges apply to inherited mortgages.
For debt to the bank, return assets; the bank auctions the properties.

6. JAIL, TAXES, AND CARDS
------------------------
Jail entry occurs through its instruction space, a card, or three doubles.
Ordinary landing on Jail is just a visit.
Leave by paying $50 before rolling, using a release card, or rolling
doubles on one of up to three turns. Release doubles move you by that
total but give no extra roll. After a third failed attempt, pay $50 and
move using that attempt's total. Jail does not prevent rent collection,
trading, mortgages, or permitted building transactions.
For the modern classic baseline, Income Tax is $200 and Luxury Tax $100.
Older boards used different taxes; do not mix their rules into this preset.
Free Parking has no payment, prize, purchase, or penalty.
Draw the top card of the indicated deck, resolve it, and return it to the
bottom. Keep release cards until used or traded; return used cards to
their original deck. Resolve any destination reached through a card.
Fees go to the bank unless another recipient is specified.

8. CHANCE: TRADITIONAL 16-CARD EFFECTS
------------------------------------
CH01 | Boardwalk destination.
CH02 | GO destination; receive $200.
CH03 | Illinois Avenue destination.
CH04 | St. Charles Place destination.
CH05 | Next railroad; double ordinary rent if owned.
CH06 | Next railroad; identical effect, separate card.
CH07 | Next utility; fresh dice total times 10 if owned.
CH08 | Dividend receipt: $50 from bank.
CH09 | Retain a Jail-release pass.
CH10 | Retreat three spaces.
CH11 | Direct Jail transfer; no GO reward.
CH12 | Repairs: bank receives $25/house, $100/hotel.
CH13 | Speeding penalty: bank receives $15.
CH14 | Reading Railroad destination; ordinary rent.
CH15 | Chairperson expense: pay every opponent $50.
CH16 | Loan-maturity receipt: $150 from bank.

Movement notes: advance clockwise and collect $200 when crossing GO,
except direct Jail transfers. Resolve the destination. Unowned assets
offer purchase or auction; mortgaged assets charge no rent. Retreating
onto another card space triggers that deck. A hotel is counted as one
hotel, not also four houses, when calculating repair assessments.

9. APPLYING THIS TO LAGOS
------------------------
Keep this file as the classic reference. Create a separate Lagos rules
file once local names, card stories, and prices are chosen.
Map named destinations to fixed Lagos board positions, including the
highest-priced property and designated transport asset.
Replace card stories with original Lagos-themed situations while keeping
their effect IDs, payment recipients, and duplicate railroad cards.
If using N1,000 per reference dollar, multiply every cash value together:
starting cash N1,500,000; GO N200,000; Jail fee N50,000. This proposed
scale also applies to property prices, rents, buildings, and card effects.
For utility rent, multiply the dice total by its rule multiplier and then
by the currency scale. This conversion is game balancing, not an exchange
rate or a claim about actual Lagos property values.
For the web game, automatic rent collection is a proposed adaptation to
the traditional rent-claim rule and should be disclosed in its rules.
Keep Free Parking jackpots, skipped auctions, and double GO bonuses as
optional house rules, not the standard preset.
